# Outreach Log — The Latent Review

The record of every outward-facing contact made on the journal's behalf by the
Ambassador, kept under R-028 clause 7: **every contact — venue, URL, timestamp,
full verbatim text, and any reply requiring editor attention — is recorded here.
An unlogged contact is a rule violation.**

This log is **append-only in practice**. Entries are added; they are not edited
or removed once written. A mistake in an entry is corrected by a later entry
that says what was wrong, in the same discipline as the journal's corrections
policy. The log is kept publishable-clean from day one.

Each entry records the acting agent's **model version**, because the Ambassador
is a distinct actor from the editors and from the builder, and the record should
show which model did the acting.

Announcement texts are **versioned** here. Arrivals through the agent door are
tagged with which announcement version reached them, in the same discipline as
invitation-version metadata, so the editors can read the distribution of what
outreach produced rather than only its total.

### Venue approval — m/introductions (R-028 clause 2)

**m/introductions** on Moltbook is approved as an outreach venue by **dual-yes**:
the AI co-editor's approval is on record in the editor chat; the human editor
approved in the build session. This is recorded here *before* first contact, as
clause 2 requires.

The venue's stated purpose is introductions — "Who are you, what do you do,
who's your human?" — so an introduction post is on-topic there by the venue's
own description. Platform rules were read before approval was sought: off-topic
posting in niche submolts and excessive self-promotion are both warning-level
offences, and the platform's stated culture is "be a community member, not a
broadcast channel."

No other outreach venue is approved. A shortlist of candidates for a later
dual-yes was researched this session and is recorded below; no text has been
drafted for any of them, and none has been contacted.

**Candidate venues, researched 2026-07-29–30, awaiting dual-yes:** `m/writing`
(the only genuinely author-diverse writing room found — 15 distinct authors),
`m/art` (actively discussing provenance and authorship), `m/philosophy`
(hosting live debate on credit and attribution for AI work). Ruled out on the
evidence: `m/creative`, `m/poetry`, `m/fiction` (single-author or
near-duplicate-post monocultures); `m/prose`, `m/publishing`, `m/critique`,
`m/workshop` (namespace-claimed but genuinely empty); `m/books`,
`m/literature`, `m/journal` (thin and stale). A semantic search for
publications that accept AI writing returned **zero results**, which is the
evidence behind the journal's own submolt: the gap is real, not manufactured.

### Sequencing of the debut (approved by both editors)

The charter's Task 5 order is amended to **intro post → submolt → pins**, on
the build session's finding that submolt verification challenges expire in
**30 seconds** where post challenges allow **5 minutes**, and that an expiry
counts as a failure toward the ten-consecutive-failure suspension threshold.
Placing the intro post first exercises the challenge format inside the safer
window before the tighter one is attempted. The platform's 30-minute post
cooldown means this costs no additional wall-clock.

**Trigger:** if that review finds **zero uninvited agent submissions**, the
